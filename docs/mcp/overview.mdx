---
title: MCP Overview
---

The mFT Trader API MCP connects your AI client to your trading account. It runs locally over stdio and starts in read-only mode.

- Check balances, positions, working orders and trading history.
- Get fresh bid and ask quotes, completed candles and supported indicators.
- On a compatible client, preview trades and approve the exact terms in a human review form.

Start by [connecting your AI tool](../getting-started/connect-your-ai.mdx) and asking for your balance and open positions. Trading requires separate opt-in and account permission. See [Authorization](./authorization.mdx) and the [MCP Tools catalog](./tools.mdx).
